Discover The Cosmos

Humanity has spent thousands of years looking at the stars and asking questions. Journey through galaxies, stars, planets, nebulae, cosmic ice and the hidden infrared universe.

🌌 Galaxies

Galaxies are enormous collections of stars, gas, dust and dark matter. Our own Milky Way contains hundreds of billions of stars.

Explore nearby galaxies and understand how these giant structures shape the universe.

⭐ Stars

Stars are powerful nuclear furnaces that create the elements needed for planets and life.

Learn how stars are born, evolve and eventually die.

☁ Nebulae

Nebulae are vast clouds of gas and dust spread across space.

Many of them serve as stellar nurseries where new stars begin their lives.

🪐 Planets

From rocky worlds to colossal gas giants, planets come in many forms.

Thousands of exoplanets have been discovered beyond our solar system.

🌈 Infrared Universe

Human eyes see only a small portion of light.

Infrared observations reveal hidden dust clouds, newborn stars and structures invisible to ordinary vision.

🧊 Ingredients For Life

Water ice and organic molecules exist throughout space.

Understanding where they form helps scientists investigate the origins of planets and potentially life itself.

🚀 Why Universe Unlocked Exists

Modern space missions collect enormous amounts of information. Much of this data is difficult for the public to understand.

Universe Unlocked transforms scientific observations into a visual, interactive and educational experience that anyone can explore.
