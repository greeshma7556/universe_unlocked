# 🌌 Universe Unlocked

## About

Universe Unlocked is an interactive astronomy platform designed to help users explore the universe through a simple and engaging experience.

The project aims to transform complex space science concepts into an accessible journey through galaxies, stars, nebulae, planets, infrared astronomy, and future NASA mission data.

---

## Features

### 🔭 Explore Objects
Browse and discover celestial objects including galaxies, stars, and nebulae.

### 🌌 Discover The Cosmos
Learn about major astronomical concepts through interactive categories.

### 🌈 Infrared Vision
Explore how the universe appears beyond visible light.

### 📊 Cosmic Fingerprints
Understand how spectra help scientists study the cosmos.

### ☄️ Time Machine
Visualize changes and movement across the universe.

### 🧊 Ingredients for Life
Learn about water ice, organic molecules, and the origins of life.

### 🤖 AI Cosmic Guide
Simplify complex astronomical concepts through easy-to-understand explanations.
